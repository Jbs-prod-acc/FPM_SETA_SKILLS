FPM SETA SKILLS FORECASTER

Manual

The Chemical Sector Occupational Outlook tool has been designed to provide a comprehensive examination of occupational trends within the chemical sector.

Features
  • Select Specialization: Use the dropdown on the left to choose a specific occupational specialization.
  • Trend Tab: Displays a time-series of the selected specialization’s data across years.
  • Correlations Tab: Shows ACF and PACF plots to explore temporal dependence.
  • Forecast Tab: Presents an ARIMA-style forecast with confidence bands and residual diagnostics (mocked here with JavaScript charts).
